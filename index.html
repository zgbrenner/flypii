    Biological wiring.
    Artificial everything else.

    The graph comes from the official FlyWire v783 proofread adult female brain release. Every proofread neuron and every positive directed connection in that release is retained. Connections appearing in multiple neuropils are summed, without removing weak edges.

    This is not a full fly nervous system, an uploaded consciousness, or a biologically validated simulation. It is a computational experiment built on the complete proofread brain wiring diagram.

    Official dataset ↗ Connectome paper ↗

    What is implemented

    Character 1–4-grams become 512 numeric features. A seeded, sparse artificial projection stimulates all neurons. A six-step recurrent rate model uses synapse counts normalized by each target’s incoming total. Counts are treated as positive strengths; transmitter signs and neuromodulation are not modeled.

    A logistic readout learns from the final and time-mean states of 512 seeded readout nodes. These are artificial readout ports, not identified motor neurons. Internal connections remain fixed.

    What the controls test

    Source-shuffled: reassigns edge sources while preserving in/out edge-count degrees and target weight totals; parallel edges are allowed. Disconnected: same neurons and projection, with recurrence removed. Encoder only: no fly network. Regex: a separate format-matching baseline.

    What it cannot establish

    Scores flag email, US-phone and SSN-shaped patterns. They cannot authenticate identifiers, locate entity spans, detect all names or addresses, establish consciousness, or justify production deployment. No evidence of biological superiority is assumed.
